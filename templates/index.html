CareGap-AI: Patient Risk & Care Gap Analytics

CareGap AI uses machine learning to analyze patient lab trends, follow-up behavior, and clinical patterns to predict health risk and detect potential care gaps early.
It helps clinicians identify patients at risk and intervene before disease progression or missed care leads to worsened outcomes.
Both real and simulated patient journeys are visualized for actionable insights.
